# Instagram Campaign Guide

## Content pillars

1. **Single Doll Portrait** — one doll rises and walks forward in a ceremonial runway scene.
2. **Imperial Pair** — Obina and Mebina-style paired presentation, used only when the photograph supports this identification.
3. **Collection Ensemble** — two or more original dolls move together without additions or removals.
4. **Cultural Detail** — close attention to garments, headpieces, accessories and symbolic meaning.
5. **From Still to Motion** — show how an original collection photograph becomes a refined seven-second film.

## Standard publishing package

Each doll receives:

- careful visual identification;
- verified English and Japanese title where possible;
- one 7-second vertical 9:16 Sora prompt;
- one concise English Instagram caption;
- one follow/view-the-collection call-to-action;
- no more than five relevant global hashtags;
- verified maker, studio, shop or dealer mentions only when evidence exists.

## Global target markets

- Japan — Tokyo

- Türkiye — Istanbul
- Mexico — Mexico City
- Indonesia — Jakarta

## Quality gate

Do not publish if:

- the doll identity has changed;
- the number of dolls is wrong;
- clothing or accessories have changed;
- a frozen source photo appears before the animation;
- duplicate characters or ghost images appear;
- wall text is misspelled;
- the caption contains an unverified historical, maker or price claim;
- more than five hashtags are used.
