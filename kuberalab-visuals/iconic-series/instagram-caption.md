# Instagram Caption Template

## Structure

**1. Hook**
One short line that names the visual idea rather than making a technical claim.

**2. Object + atmosphere**
Describe what is visible and the series mood in 1–2 sentences.

**3. KUBERALAB angle**
Connect engineering form with visual mythology without pretending the artwork is an official manufacturer campaign.

**4. Motion note**
If a Reel is used, mention the cinematic transformation from poster to motion.

**5. CTA**
Invite the viewer to follow or view the rest of the ICONIC SERIES.

## Example skeleton

> [HOOK]
>
> [VISIBLE MACHINE / VERIFIED MODEL] inside the **[SERIES]** world — [brief atmosphere].
>
> KUBERALAB explores how engineering presence can become visual mythology without losing the identity of the machine.
>
> [Optional motion sentence.]
>
> Follow for the next ICONIC SERIES study.

## Rules

- Do not invent model specifications, prices, performance figures or endorsements.
- If a model/brand is not verified, describe the visible machine generically.
- Keep hashtags relevant and limited; quality is more important than quantity.
- Do not state or imply that KUBERALAB is an official campaign partner unless that is true.
