# ICONIC SERIES — Quality Checklist

Review every poster or motion output before publication.

## Identity

- [ ] The main machine is recognisable and consistent with the supplied reference.
- [ ] No duplicate/ghost machine appears.
- [ ] Wheels, bodywork, windows, lights and visible accessories are geometrically coherent.
- [ ] No invented logo, model designation or unreadable fake text has been introduced.

## Visual direction

- [ ] The output clearly belongs to one ICONIC SERIES world.
- [ ] The machine remains the hero of the composition.
- [ ] Lighting and environment support the design rather than hiding generation defects.
- [ ] Saturn seal ♄, if present, is restrained.

## Motion

- [ ] Movement starts naturally from the established scene.
- [ ] Camera movement has one understandable purpose.
- [ ] No unexplained morphing, teleporting or extra parts appear.
- [ ] Physical cues such as reflections, dust, water, tyres and suspension behave coherently enough for the intended style.

## Claims

- [ ] Any brand/model name used is verified or supplied.
- [ ] No performance specification, price or manufacturer claim was invented.
- [ ] No sponsorship, partnership or endorsement is implied without evidence.

## Publish gate

If any identity or claims item fails, do not publish until corrected.
